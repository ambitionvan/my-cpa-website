Who this is for

Owner-operated and family businesses across Greater Vancouver — incorporated consultants, trades, retailers, restaurants and professional practices that need a T2 filed and a clear picture of what they owe and why.

How it works

1

Send your year-end records

Bank statements, receipts and invoices — paper or PDF — plus last year's T2 if you have one.

2

We prepare the statements

Your books are completed and your year-end financial statements prepared.

3

Review together

You review the return with the CPA before it is filed — including the salary vs. dividend choice.

4

File and keep the file

The T2 is filed and you keep the complete set of books and statements.

A T2 is generally due six months after your corporation's fiscal year-end. Bookkeeping is done on the cash method from the documents you provide. It is not an audit or a review engagement.

Common questions

Do I need financial statements as well as a T2?

Most small corporations need year-end statements to support the return. We prepare compilation statements as part of the year-end work.

Salary or dividends — which is better?

It depends on your income, RRSP room, CPP and your corporation's tax position. We work through the numbers with you before the return is filed.

What if CRA writes to me?

Bring the letter to us. We help you understand what CRA is asking for and prepare the reply.
