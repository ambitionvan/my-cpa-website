What is included

Monthly bookkeeping

Bookkeeping from your bank statements, month by month — so you always know where the business stands.

Every bank line, with its original

Every bank line becomes an entry, with the source document attached and traceable. At year end you receive the complete set of books on a USB key.

GST/PST returns

GST and PST returns prepared from your books, with the tax on each line picked up from the supporting document.

Payroll

Payroll for your employees, with the deductions and remittances handled as part of the monthly work.

T4 / T5 slips

Year-end T4 and T5 slips prepared from the payroll and payment records we kept during the year.

Year-end ready

Because the books are kept through the year, your year-end return is prepared from the same books. See corporate tax (T2).

How we keep your books — with DropBookAI

You hand us your papers; we book them from your bank statements, every entry with its original attached and traceable, and give you a complete set of books on a USB key.

1

Drop off

Bank statements, receipts, invoices — paper or PDF.

3

Your T2 prepared from the same books, or handed to your own CPA.

Bookkeeping is done on the cash method from the documents you provide. It is not an audit or a review engagement.

Common questions

What do I need to send each month?

Your bank statements and the receipts and invoices behind them — paper or PDF. We work from those documents.

Do I need an accountant for the basic organizing?

No. You hand the papers to your company admin; we do the booking and a CPA reviews the finished books.

Where is my data kept?

Your books are prepared locally and handed back to you as a complete set — statements, ledger, reconciliations and every original — on a USB key at year end.
