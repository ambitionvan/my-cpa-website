How it works — in 36 seconds

You drop off your papers, we book every bank line with its original attached, and the CPA reviews the finished books.

Services

Corporate tax (T2)

T2 corporate tax returns, year-end financial statements, salary vs. dividend planning for shareholders, and help with CRA reviews and letters.

Personal tax (T1)

T1 personal tax returns, foreign property reporting (T1135), non-resident property sales, and tax planning for families.

Bookkeeping, GST/PST & payroll

Monthly bookkeeping from your bank statements, GST/PST returns, payroll, and T4 / T5 slips.

How we keep your books

You hand us your papers; we book them from your bank statements, every entry with its original attached and traceable, and give you a complete set of books on a USB key. At year end we prepare your corporate tax return — or you hand the books to your own CPA.

1

Drop off

Bank statements, receipts, invoices — paper or PDF.

2

We book

Every bank line becomes an entry, with its original document attached.

3

You receive

Statements, general ledger, bank reconciliations and every original, on a USB key.

4

Year end

Your T2 prepared from the same books, or handed to your own CPA.

Bookkeeping is done on the cash method from the documents you provide. It is not an audit or a review engagement.

About the practice

Joseph Zhu, CPA provides accounting and tax services to owner-operated businesses and families across Greater Vancouver: corporate and personal tax returns, bookkeeping, and planning questions such as salary vs. dividends.

Service in English and Chinese, so the details of your taxes are explained in the language you are most comfortable with.

Easy to reach: our office is at East 8th Avenue and Rupert Street in East Vancouver — one block from East Broadway, minutes from Highway 1, and about a 5-minute walk from Rupert SkyTrain Station (Expo Line), with convenient street parking right outside. If you work in Vancouver or pass through on Highway 1, drop by to talk it over — meetings by appointment.
