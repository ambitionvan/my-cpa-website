Outsourcing Bookkeeping in Canada: What It Includes and Costs

By Joseph Zhu, CPA, CGA · Published 2026-09-01 · Bookkeeping

Managing the books is one of the most important jobs in a Canadian small business, yet it takes time owners would rather spend running the business.

What does outsourced bookkeeping include?

  • Recording transactions: income and expenses from your bank statements, with the receipt or invoice behind each one.
  • Sales tax: GST/PST returns prepared from the same books.
  • Payroll and slips: payroll runs, remittances, and year-end T4 / T5 slips.
  • Bank reconciliation: every month the books are matched to the bank statement.

How fees are usually set

Most firms charge a monthly fee based on the number of bank and card transactions, plus a separate fee for year-end statements and the corporate tax return. Ask for a written quote and a clear list of what is included.

Best practices

  • Keep one business bank account and one business card, separate from personal spending.
  • Hand over documents every month, not once a year.
  • Keep the originals: CRA generally expects records to be kept for six years.
