Common Myths About Outsourced Bookkeeping, Dispelled

By Joseph Zhu, CPA, CGA · Published 2026-06-15 · Myths

A few misconceptions keep owners from getting help with their books.

Myth 1: "I will lose control of my finances."
In practice you get monthly statements and a bank reconciliation, so you see more, not less.

Myth 2: "Outsourcing is only for big companies."
Fees are based on your volume, which suits small and new businesses.

Myth 3: "My documents will be everywhere."
Ask your firm where records are kept and who can see them, and get it in writing.
