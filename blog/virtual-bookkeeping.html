Paperless Bookkeeping: Why Digital Records Make Life Easier

By Joseph Zhu, CPA, CGA · Published 2026-07-01 · Technology

The shoebox of paper receipts is giving way to digital records that are easier to keep and easier to find.

Benefits of digital records

  • Find anything quickly: every entry can link to the scanned original behind it.
  • Nothing gets lost: scans are kept with backups, so a faded receipt is not a lost deduction.
  • Easier CRA questions: when CRA asks for support, the documents are ready to send.
