Outsourced Bookkeeping vs. an In-House Bookkeeper

By Joseph Zhu, CPA, CGA · Published 2026-06-01 · Comparison

Should you hire a bookkeeper or work with an accounting firm? A simple comparison:

  • In-house bookkeeper: salary, employer CPP/EI, vacation pay, benefits, desk space, software licences, and someone to review the work.
  • Accounting firm: a fee for the work actually needed, with a CPA reviewing the books; no hiring or turnover to manage.

For most businesses with a few hundred transactions a month or fewer, an outside firm is usually the lower-cost option.
