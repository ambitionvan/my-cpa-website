The Real Cost of Doing Your Own Books

By Joseph Zhu, CPA, CGA · Published 2026-07-15 · Planning

Doing the books yourself, or leaving them to year end, can look cheaper. The hidden costs often say otherwise.

Hidden costs

  • Late filing penalties and interest: late GST/PST or T2 filings and payments attract CRA penalties and daily compound interest.
  • Missed deductions: without the receipt behind an expense, it is hard to support the deduction.
  • Your time: hours sorting receipts are hours not spent on customers.
