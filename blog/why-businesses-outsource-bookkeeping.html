Why More Canadian Businesses Outsource Their Bookkeeping

By Joseph Zhu, CPA, CGA · Published 2026-08-01 · Business

More owners are choosing to keep a lean team and have the books done outside.

The main reasons

  1. Lower fixed cost: no salary, CPP/EI employer share or benefits for an in-house bookkeeper.
  2. It grows with you: the service scales with the number of transactions.
  3. Tax questions answered early: the person keeping the books can flag tax issues during the year, not after it.
