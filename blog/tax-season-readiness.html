Tax Season Readiness: How Organized Books Save You Money

By Joseph Zhu, CPA, CGA · Published 2026-05-15 · Tax

Tax season does not have to be stressful. Books kept up during the year make the T2 faster and the year-end fee lower.

Three habits

  1. Keep a digital copy of every receipt and invoice.
  2. Reconcile the bank and credit card statements every month.
  3. Keep business and personal accounts completely separate.
