How to Find a Good Accountant for Your Business in Canada

By Joseph Zhu, CPA, CGA · Published 2026-08-15 · CPA advice

A good accountant does more than file returns at year end — they help you understand your numbers and plan ahead.

What to look for

  1. Professional designation: a Canadian CPA is held to a code of professional conduct and continuing education. You can check a CPA's membership with the provincial body (CPABC in British Columbia).
  2. Clear communication: an advisor who speaks your language explains tax rules more clearly.
  3. Planning, not just filing: for example, how to pay yourself — salary, dividends or a mix — and when instalments are due.
  4. A written engagement letter: what is included, what is not, and the fee.
