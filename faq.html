My corporation has not filed tax returns for several years. Can you help?

Yes. We help corporations catch up on unfiled years, prepare the missing returns and financial statements, and work directly with CRA. For example, we helped a client whose company was incorporated in 2021 and had never filed: we completed the 2021 to 2025 returns and the client avoided further legal action by CRA.

I received a letter from CRA. What should I do?

Do not ignore it. Bring the letter to us and we will help you understand what CRA is asking for, what the deadline is, and prepare the reply.

What is T1135 and do I need to file it?

T1135 is the CRA form for reporting specified foreign property. You generally need to file it if you own specified foreign property with a total cost of more than CAD 100,000 at any time during the year. If you are unsure whether your property counts, bring the details and we will check.

Salary or dividends — which is better for an owner-manager?

It depends on your income, RRSP room, CPP and your corporation's tax position. We work through the numbers with you before the return is filed, so you can decide with the full picture.

How does your bookkeeping work?

We book from your bank statements: every bank line becomes an entry, with its original document attached. Bookkeeping is done on-device with AI, fully offline, and reviewed by a CPA. At year end you receive a complete set of books, and your T2 can be prepared from the same books.
